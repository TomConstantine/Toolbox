    HEIC photos are decoded and downloaded as JPEG, since browsers can't write HEIC directly; metadata is carried across on a best-effort basis for HEIC, and reliably for JPEG. This tool helps you obscure identifiable faces and plates before sharing a photo, but it isn't legal advice and can't guarantee UK GDPR compliance on its own — check your redactions carefully, and consider whether the image needs further review before you publish it. Automatic face detection is a starting point, not a guarantee; it works best on clear, front-facing faces, so always check its results yourself and add boxes by hand for anything it misses.
